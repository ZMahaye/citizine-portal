Legal Framework

Public transport operating licenses in KZN are governed by:

  • National Land Transport Act (NLTA), No. 5 of 2009 – governs operating licenses for land transport.
  • KwaZulu-Natal Transport Authority (KZNTA) – responsible for issuing and regulating operating licenses.
  • Road Traffic Act, No. 93 of 1996 – roadworthiness and vehicle fitness standards.

For in-person applications, visit your nearest KZN DOT Regional Office or Transport Authority offices in your district. Enquiries: 0800 203 803 (toll-free) | info@kzntransport.gov.za
